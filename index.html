Seek Research was built to solve what others couldn't — the hidden, tangled, and too-often forgotten properties that stall progress and block community potential.

Families are unaware they're entitled to an inheritance, leaving generational wealth untouched. Developers can't build on what isn't claimed. For Heirs & Families

Why did Seek Research contact me?+

Our research identified a property that may have a connection to your family — most often through a relative who purchased land years or decades ago. We reach out to families when we believe there may be an inheritance opportunity they're unaware of. If we contacted you, it's because we have specific reason to believe you may have a legal interest in a property.

Is there any cost to me or my family?+

No — there is absolutely no upfront cost to families, ever. Seek Research covers all research and coordination costs ourselves. We are only reimbursed at closing, from the proceeds of the property sale. If nothing happens, you owe us nothing.

How do I know this is legitimate?+

That's a completely fair question to ask. Seek Research is a registered firm based in Philadelphia, PA, and a member of the American Land Title Association (ALTA). We work directly with licensed probate attorneys and title companies — not around them. We're happy to provide references and documentation about who we are before any commitment is made.

What happens if I decide not to participate?+

That's entirely your right and we'll respect it. There's no pressure and no obligation. If you choose not to proceed, we simply close the file. We do encourage you to at least have one conversation with us first — just to understand what may be available to your family before making a decision.

How long does the process take?+

It depends on the complexity of the case. Simple files can close in a few months. Cases that require probate court proceedings typically take 6–18 months. We keep you informed at every stage through our secure client portal, so you're never left wondering where things stand.

What is probate, and do I need it?+

Probate is the legal process through which a deceased person's estate — including real property — is transferred to their heirs. Not every case requires it, but many do, especially when properties were owned by someone who passed away without a will. If probate is needed, we connect you with trusted attorneys and walk you through the process step by step.

For Title Companies & Attorneys

What types of files does Seek Research handle?+

We specialize in complex cases involving unclear ownership, missing or multiple heirs, tangled titles, deed fraud, and properties that have been dormant for decades. If a file looks too complicated or too risky for standard processing, that's exactly the kind of case we're built for.

How do you deliver your research findings?+

All research is delivered through our secure client portal, formatted to support legal filings and title insurance requirements. Documentation is sourced, organized, and ready for court — minimizing the need for your team to do additional verification work.

Do you work with probate attorneys directly?+

Yes — probate attorneys are one of our core partner groups. We provide court-ready heir research and historical ownership documentation formatted specifically to support legal filings. We work alongside your team, not in place of it, and can coordinate directly with your office throughout the file.

How do we get started working with Seek Research?+

The easiest way is to schedule an initial consultation through our contact page. We'll discuss your current workflow, the types of files you're working with, and how Seek Research can fit into your process. From there, we can move quickly — most engagements begin within days of our first conversation.

For Developers & Impact Investors

Can you help me identify specific properties in a target area?+

Yes. If you bring us a target neighborhood or set of criteria, we can research dormant properties in that area — identifying ownership status, heir information, and potential paths to clear title. You can also come to us with a specific address and we'll dig into the ownership history.

What does "clear title" mean, and why does it matter for development?+

Clear title means the property has a legally verifiable, uncontested owner — someone who can lawfully sell or transfer it. Without clear title, a property cannot be sold, developed, or insured. Tangled or unclear title is one of the most common reasons development projects stall before they even begin.

How does Seek Research support community-centered development?+

We're committed to ensuring that property resolution benefits the families and communities involved — not just investors. We work with impact-focused partners to ensure that rightful heirs are properly compensated, that acquisitions are handled with transparency, and that development plans reflect the needs of the neighborhoods they're entering.
